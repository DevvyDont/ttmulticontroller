# Multicontroller v2.0.3

A small update on top of the 2.0 rebuild. Grab it in-app from About > Check for updates, or download the exe below; your settings carry over automatically.

## New in 2.0.3
- Game-window title bars can now be tinted with your Windows accent color, as an alternative to the current mode color (Settings > Appearance > Colors). The two title-bar tints are mutually exclusive, so pick whichever you prefer.
- Interface wording now consistently spells it "color" (was "colour").

---

Everything from the 2.0 rebuild is still here:

> Note: the initial 2.0.0 build failed to launch (the download was missing some bundled files); it is now a genuinely standalone single .exe.

## A whole new look
- Completely redesigned, modern (Windows11-style) interface: a cleaner main window and an organized Settings window grouped into clear categories.
- New app logo, a yin-yang of two cats that takes on your Multi and Mirror mode colors.

## Better game-window borders
- Adjustable border thickness and corner rounding.
- Smooth, anti-aliased borders and numbers, no more jagged edges or colored fringe.
- Optional per-window group numbers, with settings for when they appear.

## Enhanced Layout Presets & Custom Modes (BETA)
- Layout Presets is now one page with a live preview, a monitor picker, and on-screen per-window adjustment (with a pop-out preview).
- Custom Modes rebuilt into a slightly simpler mode picker with plain-language rules.

## Keybinds and Input
- Potentially fixed sticky keys across mode switches, window swaps, and custom modes.
- Ability to set your own mode-switch key (default is Alt)
- Precise (previously named Controlled Multi-Click) mode no longer hijacks your normal clicks, and toggle/hold is consistent.
- All Multi-click settings are now found in the "Multi-Click" tab.
- More reliable held-key release, event-driven window tracking, and clear warnings if a hotkey can't/doesn't register.

## Also in 2.0
- Single portable `.exe`, no installer and no separate .NET download required.
- One-click updates: "Check for updates" now downloads and installs the new version for you, then restarts (no more manual download).
- Sharper on high-DPI displays, with better keyboard accessibility throughout.

## How to update (First Timers)
- Download `Multicontroller.exe` below and replace your existing copy. Your settings (keybinds, custom modes, layout presets) live next to the app and are kept automatically.
- For future updates, just hit "Check for updates" in About: it downloads and installs the update for you and reopens on the new version.
