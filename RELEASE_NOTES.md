# Multicontroller v2.0.4

A small update on top of the 2.0 rebuild. Grab it in-app from About > Check for updates, or download the exe below; your settings carry over automatically.

## New in 2.0.4
- Fixed a long-standing layout-preset bug on multi-monitor setups where the monitors use different display scales (for example one at 100% and one at 125% in Windows Settings > Display > Scale). Applying a preset would stretch the windows in the column nearest the higher-scaled monitor, making them taller and taller down each row. Presets now place every window at the exact size and position you laid out, no matter what scale your other monitors use.

---

## How to update (First Timers)
- Download `Multicontroller.exe` below and replace your existing copy. Your settings (keybinds, custom modes, layout presets) live next to the app and are kept automatically.
- For future updates, just hit "Check for updates" in About: it downloads and installs the update for you and reopens on the new version.
