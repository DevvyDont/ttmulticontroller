# Multicontroller v2.0.5

A small update on top of the 2.0 rebuild. Grab it in-app from About > Check for updates, or download the exe below; your settings carry over automatically.

## New in 2.0.5
- **New "Dormant" mode** in the Switching mode category in Window Management settings. Use this mode for a window you don't want to receive multi/mirror inputs while still being connected to the multicontroller.

## Fixes & improvements
- **Warning labels no longer get clipped.** When global hotkeys are suspended or the mode is locked, the controller window now grows just enough to fit the "Hotkeys suspended" / "Mode locked" indicators instead of cutting them off — in both the normal and compact layouts. The two indicators also show and hide independently, and compact mode uses shorter labels so both fit.

---

## How to update (First Timers)
- Download `Multicontroller.exe` below and replace your existing copy. Your settings (keybinds, custom modes, layout presets) live next to the app and are kept automatically.
- For future updates, just hit "Check for updates" in About: it downloads and installs the update for you and reopens on the new version.
